Broke Student Mode

When the numbers don't look good, this mode focuses on damage control and small wins, not perfection.

Priority Moves
If You're Short This Month
  • Lock in essentials: rent, utilities, basic food.
  • Pause or cancel "nice-to-have" subscriptions.
  • Look for quick, realistic income bumps (extra shifts, side gigs).
Mindset
You're Not Failing

Being broke in college is common. The win is tracking honestly and making one small move at a time.

  • Try a "no-spend" weekend challenge.
  • Swap outings for free campus events.
  • Ask for help early (advisors, support offices) instead of waiting.

About ZoomiBudgets_Beta_V2

Student-first

Designed for students practicing real-world money skills: budgets, bills, and saving for stuff that actually matters.

Local only

All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. No banking connections and no server – just your laptop, your numbers, and your habits.

Future-ready

The same structure can later plug into a real database or bank APIs. For now, it's a safe sandbox to learn without risking real accounts.

Why budgeting matters

Budgeting isn’t about being perfect. It’s about giving every dollar a job before it disappears. When you know what’s coming in and where it’s going:

  • You catch money leaks (impulse food, random subscriptions) before they snowball.
  • You notice when bills or debt are crowding out everything else.
  • You can carve out a savings cushion, even if it’s tiny at first.
  • You stop “guessing” if you can afford something – the numbers tell you.

ZoomiBudgets is built so budgeting feels less like a punishment and more like a game: colorful charts, a score, goals to level up, and clear feedback. The point isn’t to never spend – it’s to spend on purpose.

Export / import (for class & collaboration)

Use this to share your budget with teammates or submit a data file for a class project. Everything stays in JSON, and nothing leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

Beta feature

Tip: You can export your data, wipe it, and then re-import later to simulate “cloud sync” in a classroom setting.
